MOTM Team Structure

Operations Team – Executes client campaigns and revenue-generation activities.
Sales Team – Lead follow-up, qualification and conversion; currently supported by a shared Sales Engineer.
Marketing & Digital Team – Email marketing, WhatsApp marketing, content and brand awareness.
Data Research Team – Builds and validates Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) databases.
Calling / AI Calling Team – Executes outbound calling and lead qualification.
SEO & Content Team – SEO, PSEO and content activities for organic visibility and lead generation.
Key Account Executives – Coordinate with clients, track team performance and manage campaign requirements.

Key Limitations / Strategic Considerations
Lead quality directly impacts conversion and revenue.
Client response time can affect lead conversion.
Sales capacity can become a bottleneck when lead volume increases.
Data accuracy and freshness need continuous validation.
Client product-market fit, pricing and positioning influence conversion rates.
Technical products require proper client training before outreach.
Outreach channels have platform, email and calling limitations.
SEO/PSEO and organic channels require time to generate consistent results.
Multiple teams require strong coordination to avoid delays and lead leakage.
Lead generation ≠ revenue; leads need qualification, follow-up and sales conversion.
Client approvals and changes can impact campaign execution.
Market and geography can significantly affect campaign performance.
Core Objective

MOTM's primary objective is not simply to generate leads, but to create a complete lead-to-revenue pipeline for clients — from identifying prospects and generating leads to nurturing, conversion and ultimately generating business.

MOTM Process After Client Onboarding
Understand Client Requirements
Understand the client's product/service, target market, ICP, goals and revenue objectives.
Client Training
Take technical/product training to understand features, use cases, FAQs and prospect objections.
Planning & Strategy
Define lead targets, target companies, outreach channels, resources, timelines and expected outcomes.
Plan Approval
Share the proposed strategy and execution plan with the client and incorporate feedback/approval.
Team Allocation
Assign Sales, Data Research, Calling, Marketing, Digital, SEO/PSEO and other required resources.
Target Database Creation
Research and build the target company/prospect database based on the approved ICP.
Data Validation & Client Approval
Validate contact information and share the target database with the client where required.
Outreach Preparation
Prepare:
Cold-calling scripts
Email sequences
WhatsApp communication
LinkedIn outreach
AI calling flows
Outreach Execution
Start calling, emailing, LinkedIn/WhatsApp outreach and other agreed activities.
Lead Qualification
Identify interested prospects, understand requirements and qualify them based on the client's criteria.
Lead Handover & Follow-up
Share qualified leads with the appropriate sales/client team and coordinate follow-ups.
Lead-to-Revenue Conversion
Sales team works on proposals, discussions, negotiations and conversion into actual business.
Digital & Organic Growth
SEO, PSEO, content, website and social media activities support continuous visibility and lead generation.
Performance Tracking
Track outreach, responses, qualified leads, meetings, conversions and revenue-related metrics through MIS.
Weekly Client Review
Review performance, challenges, lead quality, conversions and upcoming activities with the client.
Strategy Optimization
Based on performance, refine the ICP, messaging, channels, outreach approach and resource allocation.
Continuous Lead-to-Revenue Cycle
Repeat the process continuously, with the primary focus on generating qualified opportunities and converting them into revenue for the client.
